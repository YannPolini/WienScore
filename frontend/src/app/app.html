How the grade works

{{ result.profile }} profile. {{ result.profileDescription }}

Each measurement falls continuously from 100 at zero distance to 80 at its benchmark, then to zero at three times that distance. Meeting a benchmark does not mean a perfect score. The 80-point reference is a design choice for comparing proximity, not a measured effect on wellbeing. Green-space and healthcare measurements are averaged. Transport uses the better bus/tram or rail score. Profile weights combine the factors.

The weighted contributions add up to the overall score before rounding. Grade cutoffs: 80 → 1, 60 → 2, 40 → 3, 20 → 4, below 20 → 5. Grades use unrounded values. Displayed numbers may differ slightly when added.

Green-space targets follow Vienna’s planning benchmarks. The 500 m bus/tram and 1,000 m rail proxies are informed by UN guidance, which uses walking routes. This app uses straight-line distance. Healthcare and library targets, weights, decline and grade cutoffs are design choices.
